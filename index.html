The house ↓

A masseria is a farmhouse that learned to keep guests. We kept the stone, the shade, and the silence.

The house has been in the family since 1968. We restored it room by room, with masons from Ostuni and a stubborn refusal to hurry. We added better beds.

The house

Limestone, oak, lime plaster. Nine rooms open onto the courtyard; none has a television.

There is a long table under the fig tree, a pool the colour of the sky at noon, and a kitchen that smells of bread by seven. The rest of the house is for reading, sleeping, and walking to the sea.
