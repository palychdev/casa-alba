Type

Fraunces by Undercase Type is used for display. Instrument Sans by Rodrigo Fuenzalida is used for text. Both are self-hosted and licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

The property

Casa Alba is a fictional masseria, invented for this design study. It is not a real hotel and cannot be booked. The address, rates, and menu are part of the fiction. Any resemblance to a real house is a coincidence.

The enquiry form is a planner: it validates dates and keeps a summary on the page. It does not send anything to a server, and there is no email address or mail app involved. There are no cookies, trackers, or analytics on this site.

Build

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Photographs were cropped, converted to AVIF and WebP, and served at three widths. The source is on GitHub.
